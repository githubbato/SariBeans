OUR STORY

About Our Coffee Shop
in Angeles City

Sari-Sari Bean opened in Balibago, Angeles City with a mission to bring authentic Filipino coffee culture to our community. Our coffee shop near System Plus College has quickly become a favorite gathering spot for students, professionals, and families throughout Angeles City.

The Philippines has been growing coffee since the 1740s, and our mountains produce some of the world's finest Arabica and Liberica beans. We're proud to bring this heritage to life in every cup served at our Balibago location.

Conveniently located beside Abacan Bridge in Balibago, we're easy to find whether you're a local resident, a student from System Plus College, or visiting from Clark Freeport. Our mission is to celebrate Filipino coffee culture while supporting local farmers through direct trade partnerships.

Why We Source Philippine Coffee: Supporting Local Farmers

📅 March 1, 2026⏱️ 2 min read✍️ Michael Usi Jr

At Sari-Sari Bean, we're proud to source 100% of our coffee from Philippine farmers. This decision wasn't just about quality—it was about supporting our local farming communities.

Direct Trade Relationships

We work directly with coffee farmers in Benguet, Sagada, and Batangas. By eliminating middlemen, we ensure fair prices that help farmers invest in their farms and families.

Our direct trade model means:

  • Fair wages for farmers and their workers
  • Investment in sustainable farming practices
  • Better quality beans through careful handling
  • Traceability from farm to cup

Quality You Can Taste

Philippine coffee has a unique flavor profile that reflects our country's diverse terroir. From the bold, earthy notes of Barako to the smooth, nutty flavors of Arabica grown in the cool mountains of Benguet.

Supporting Philippine Agriculture

By choosing Philippine coffee, we're helping to sustain a legacy of coffee growing that dates back to the Spanish colonial era.

Taste the Difference of Local

Experience our locally-sourced coffees at our Balibago location in Angeles City.

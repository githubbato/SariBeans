The Art of Filipino Barako: A Heritage Coffee Experience

📅 March 1, 2026⏱️ 2 min read✍️ Isaiah Talens

Barako coffee is more than just a beverage—it's a piece of Philippine history. This bold, aromatic coffee has been a morning staple in Filipino households for generations.

What is Barako?

Barako refers to coffee beans from the Liberica variety, grown primarily in the Philippines. Known for its large beans and distinctive smoky flavor, Barako is a favorite among coffee connoisseurs.

Our Barako Selection

At Sari-Sari Bean, we source our Barako from trusted farmers in Batangas and Cavite. Try it in various forms:

  • Traditional hot brew
  • Barako latte
  • Ice Barako

Try Our Barako

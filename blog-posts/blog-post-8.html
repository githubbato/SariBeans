Sustainability

Sustainable Coffee Practices at Sari-Sari Bean

📅 March 3, 2026⏱️ 3 min read✍️ Gian Pangilinan

Sustainability isn't just a buzzword at Sari-Sari Bean—it's a commitment we take seriously in serving the Angeles City community.

Local Sourcing

By sourcing our beans from Philippine farmers, we reduce the carbon footprint associated with transportation. Supporting local agriculture is better for the environment.

Eco-Friendly Packaging

We've transitioned to compostable cups and straws. Every small change adds up to make a bigger impact on our environment.

Waste Reduction

  • Coffee grounds are composted and given to local farmers
  • We encourage reusable cups with discounts
  • Food waste is minimized through careful planning

Sustainable Coffee in Angeles City

Join us in our commitment to sustainable coffee practices. Visit us in Balibago today!

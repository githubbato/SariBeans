Behind the Scenes: Our Baristas' Story

📅 March 3, 2026⏱️ 2 min read✍️ Mary Tingin

Our baristas are the heart and soul of Sari-Sari Bean. Today, we're introducing you to the talented team behind your favorite coffee.

Meet Our Team

Our baristas come from diverse backgrounds but share a common passion for coffee. From students to professionals, each team member brings their unique story to our shop.

Training Excellence

We invest in our team through comprehensive training:

  • Coffee brewing techniques
  • Milk texturing and latte art
  • Customer service excellence
  • Food safety standards

Career Growth

We're committed to helping our team grow in the coffee industry. Many of our baristas have advanced to supervisory roles and continue to develop their skills.

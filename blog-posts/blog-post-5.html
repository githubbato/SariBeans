Our Signature Latte Creations: From Pandan to Ube

📅 March 2, 2026⏱️ 3 min read✍️ Joshua Lomocso

At Sari-Sari Bean, we love putting a Filipino twist on classic coffee drinks. Our signature lattes celebrate the unique flavors of the Philippines.

Pandan Latte

Our Pandan Latte features the sweet, vanilla-like aroma of pandan leaves—a staple in Filipino cuisine. The green, fragrant pandan adds a unique local touch to your favorite latte.

Ube Latte

Made with purple yam (ube), this latte offers a creamy, slightly sweet flavor that's distinctly Filipino. The vibrant purple color makes it as beautiful as it is delicious.

Mango Latte

Perfect for the tropical climate of Pampanga, our Mango Latte combines the sweetness of ripe Philippine mangoes with smooth espresso.

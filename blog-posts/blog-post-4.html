How We Roast: Small Batch Excellence in Angeles City

📅 March 1, 2026⏱️ 2 min read✍️ Isaiah Talens

Great coffee is made in the roast. At Sari-Sari Bean, we take roasting seriously. Every batch is carefully monitored to ensure perfection in every cup.

Our Roasting Philosophy

We believe in small-batch roasting because it allows us to maintain consistent quality and freshness. Each batch is roasted to bring out the unique characteristics of the beans.

The Roasting Process

  • Green bean inspection - Every batch is hand-selected
  • Precision roasting - Temperature and timing are carefully monitored
  • Cooling and resting - Beans are allowed to develop their full flavor
  • Fresh packaging - Sealed immediately to preserve freshness
